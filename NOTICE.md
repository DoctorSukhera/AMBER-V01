# AMBER V0.1 — Research and IP Notice

## Research status

AMBER V0.1 is a research-software prototype for molecular-to-pathology workflow development. The current displayed score uses placeholder coefficients for interface demonstration only.

No clinical performance metrics, diagnostic claims, model-lock date, operating thresholds, sensitivity, specificity, AUROC, or other validation results should be inferred from V0.1.

## Intended endpoint

The intended research endpoint is cerebral amyloid positivity/pathology, preferably anchored to amyloid PET in the planned clinical validation design. AMBER is not presently a stand-alone Alzheimer's disease diagnosis.

## Intellectual-property boundary

Potentially novel DNA Compass engineering details are deliberately excluded from the public repository, including exact sequences, architecture dimensions, fabrication parameters, recognition chemistry, and other unpublished implementation details.

## Clinical and privacy boundary

Do not use AMBER V0.1 for patient care. Do not upload identifiable patient information or private hospital datasets to this repository.
